02

Monthly point calculation

At the end of each monthly period, the loyalty engine evaluates all active programs for every eligible outlet. The calculation produces a final points value based on actual performance against the configured targets and criteria.

Calculation grace period

Not all invoices arrive on the final day of a period. Each loyalty program can be configured with a grace period — a set number of days after the period closes during which late invoices are still captured. Final points are confirmed only once this window expires.

Example

The June period ends on June 30. With a 3-day grace period, invoices dated within June that arrive on July 1, 2, or 3 are still counted. The final calculation is confirmed on July 3.

Points in process

As soon as the monthly calculation is complete, earned points are immediately visible to the outlet in the loyalty app as "Points in Process" — before the approval cycle and before the wallet is credited. This gives outlets full, real-time visibility of what they have earned without waiting for the process to finish.

Tier status

Tier eligibility is evaluated using calculated points directly. In-process points count toward tier status regardless of where the approval process stands.

03

Approval mechanism

For programs that require it, an approval step sits between the calculation and the wallet credit. Before any points reach an outlet's wallet, the central team reviews and confirms the calculated amounts — giving the business full control before points become redeemable.

How requests are generated

When the system is ready to credit points for a program that has approval configured, it automatically generates an approval request. One request is created per outlet per program per month, with no manual trigger needed. Requests regenerate automatically every cycle.

Request details
  • 1 request per outlet per program
  • Generated automatically each month
  • No expiry — stays pending until acted on
  • Cannot be edited or rejected
What the approver reviews
  • Program name and period
  • Outlet name and distributor
  • Total achievement for the period
  • Total points to be credited
  • Expandable criteria-level breakdown

Multi-level approval

1
Request enters Stage 1 queue
The Stage 1 approver sees the request in their Pending view. They can review all achievement data and the criteria breakdown before acting.
2
Stage 1 approves → moves to Stage 2
Once Stage 1 approves, the request becomes visible to Stage 2 (where configured). Only approval is possible — requests cannot be modified or declined at any stage.
3
All stages complete
Once the final approval stage is cleared, the request is marked as fully approved and the wallet credit fires automatically.
4
Points credited to outlet wallet
Approved points are added to the outlet's wallet. If Credit Note conversion is enabled on the program, the CN flow triggers immediately after.
Key constraint

Approval requests cannot be edited or rejected. Any discrepancies should be raised through the standard support process. Requests have no expiry and remain in the pending queue until all approval stages are cleared.

Approver views

View What it shows
Pending Requests at the approver's current stage awaiting action. Sortable by program and month.
Approved Requests that have passed all stages. Historical view — no further action possible.

04

Credit Note (CN) conversion

CN conversion is an optional setting at the program level. Only programs with CN conversion enabled trigger the Credit Note flow after wallet credit. Programs without this setting leave points in the outlet's wallet as redeemable points — nothing further happens.

Conversion rate

The monetary value of a Credit Note is derived from the credited points using a conversion rate defined in the reward master configuration. A single fixed rate applies per reward type per company — for example, 1 point = ₹1. The CN amount is calculated at the moment of conversion and is permanently fixed on the CN record.

What happens at conversion

Two accounting entries are recorded simultaneously the moment conversion fires:

✓  Points credited
  • Approved points added to the outlet wallet
  • Wallet balance updated to reflect earned points
  • Linked back to the source program and criteria
−  Points redeemed as CN
  • Same points immediately redeemed
  • Recorded as "Converted to Credit Note"
  • Net wallet balance change = zero
Net effect on wallet

Because the credit and the redemption are equal, the outlet's wallet balance does not increase. The points are converted directly into a Credit Note of equivalent monetary value. The outlet sees the CN in their CN section — not as spendable points.

Credit Note details

Field Value at creation
CN amountCalculated from points × conversion rate defined in the reward master
OutletThe outlet against which the points were earned
DistributorThe distributor mapped to the outlet — responsible for settlement
Source programThe loyalty program under which the points were earned
PeriodThe month in which the points were earned
StatusPending — updated to Settled by DMS upon settlement
05

DMS integration & settlement

Once a Credit Note is created in the loyalty system, it is made available to the Distributor Management System (DMS). DMS is responsible for applying the CN value against the relevant outlet invoice and reporting settlement status back.

1
Credit Note created
Status is set to Pending. The CN contains the amount, outlet details, distributor, source program, and the earning period.
2
DMS retrieves pending Credit Notes
DMS calls the loyalty system to retrieve all pending CNs for its distributors. DMS maps each CN to the appropriate outlet account internally.
3
Distributor applies CN to next invoice
The CN value is deducted from the next invoice raised by the distributor for that outlet. The outlet's payable is reduced by the CN amount.
4
DMS confirms settlement
DMS sends a settlement confirmation back to the loyalty system. The CN status updates to Settled and the settlement reference is stored for audit.

Integration approach

Direction Type Purpose
DMS → Loyalty GET Retrieve pending Credit Notes for a distributor or outlet
DMS → Loyalty POST Confirm CN settlement with invoice reference
06

Outlet experience

The outlet interacts with two dedicated sections in the loyalty app that reflect the complete journey of their earned points.

Points in process

As soon as the monthly calculation is complete, the outlet sees their earned points displayed as "Points in Process" against the relevant program — before the approval cycle and before the wallet is credited. This gives outlets a transparent, real-time view of what they have earned.

Credit Note tracking

Once a CN is generated, it appears in the outlet's dedicated CN section in the loyalty app. An outlet can have multiple CNs active simultaneously from different months or different programs — each tracked independently.

Status What the outlet sees
Pending Credit Note has been issued. The distributor has not yet applied it against an invoice. CN amount is visible.
Settled The CN has been applied by the distributor. Settlement is complete and no further action is required.
